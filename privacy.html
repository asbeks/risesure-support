RiseSure privacy policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026

RiseSure is an offline alarm app published by the Fulbek developer. It does not require an account and does not contain advertising or application analytics.

Camera and movement

With your permission, the app processes live camera frames on your phone to detect a face, head movements, eye opening and body movements for a wake-up challenge. Bundled Google ML Kit models are available from installation without a network download. Frames remain in memory and are discarded after processing. RiseSure does not save photographs or videos, build face templates, recognize identity, or transmit camera frames. The camera is used only while the challenge screen is visible. The app does not have Android internet permission.

These checks cannot medically establish wakefulness and are not biometric authentication or certified protection against photo or video replay. A non-camera thinking challenge is available. Exercise is optional and should not be used when unsuitable for your health or surroundings.

Data on your phone

Alarm times, names, repeat days, challenge preferences, active wake-up sessions and up to 180 morning records are stored in private device storage. Android cloud backup is disabled for this app. To delete this data, clear RiseSure's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Removing data or uninstalling also removes scheduled alarms.

Permissions

- Exact alarms: ring at your selected time, including while the phone is idle.
- Notifications and full-screen alarms: show the alarm and its wake-up challenge.
- Foreground playback, wake lock and vibration: play alarm audio reliably while it is ringing.
- Camera: process the selected wake-up challenge.
- Receive boot completed: restore saved alarms after a restart.

No contacts, location, microphone or photo-library access is requested. A full-screen alarm may appear over the lock screen; Android may require unlocking before the camera can be used.

Google Play

The app asks the Google Play Store service whether an update is available. Google Play may process installation and update information under Google's own privacy policy. Update checks are separate from offline alarms. RiseSure does not operate a server that receives your alarm or camera data.

Safety and control

Emergency stop is always available. System volume controls, force-stop, power-off and uninstall remain controlled by Android. An app cannot guarantee an alarm on a powered-off or force-stopped device. Test it on your own phone before relying on it.
